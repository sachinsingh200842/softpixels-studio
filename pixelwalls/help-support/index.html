Help & Support

Need help with PixelWalls? Email sachinsingh200842@gmail.com. Include your device model, Android version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or a child’s date of birth.

Common questions

How do I request account deletion?

Email us from the address associated with your Google sign-in and use the subject “PixelWalls account deletion”. See the account deletion page for details.

How do I manage notifications?

Change PixelWalls notification permission in your Android device’s Settings. Names of settings can vary by device.

How do I manage ad privacy?

Use the ad privacy message shown in the app when available, Android advertising settings, or Google ad settings. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

How do I report a wallpaper rights concern?

Email the image page or URL, explain your relationship to the content, and describe the concern. We will review the information.
