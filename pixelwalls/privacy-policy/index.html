PIXELWALLS · INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelWalls, an Android wallpaper application operated by SoftPixels (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the app. The app lets you browse wallpapers, save favorites, sign in to account features, and receive notifications.

Information we handle

  • Account details: If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication processes an account identifier and the profile information provided by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile image.
  • Favorites and profile data: If you use account features, we store your account identifier and the favorites and profile information needed to sync your experience. Favorites used without an account remain on your device.
  • Age range: The app asks for a date of birth to determine feature availability. The date is checked on your device and discarded; only a broad age band is saved in local app storage. It is not sent to us.
  • Notifications: If notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device token so the app can deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • Diagnostics and performance: Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance may process crash reports, app performance measurements, device/app information, and diagnostic events to help maintain the app.
  • Advertising: PixelWalls uses Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google may process identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), ad interactions, and technical information to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, and prevent fraud. PixelWalls does not request GPS location for advertising. Advertising providers may use device storage, advertising identifiers and similar technologies to support ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The information processed may depend on your device settings, region and consent choices.
  • Service and network data: The app requests wallpaper catalog and image files from Firebase and Cloudflare-hosted services. Those providers may process IP address, request, device, and log data to deliver and secure the services.

Children and age-based features

PixelWalls is designed for a mixed-age audience. Users under 13 may browse wallpapers without Google sign-in or ads; their favorites stay on the device. Account features are available to users aged 13 and over. For eligible teen users, age-treatment signals may be sent to Google with ad or consent requests. The exact date of birth is not sent to PixelWalls or its providers.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate account and favorite syncing, deliver wallpapers and notifications, show and measure ads, respond to support requests, maintain security, and diagnose reliability or performance issues. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with the service providers described above only as needed for their services, and may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy and security, How Google uses information from apps, and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can use browsing features without signing in. You can sign out, manage Android notification permissions, and use Android or Google advertising settings to reset or manage ad personalization. Where a version of PixelWalls presents Google’s advertising privacy message, you can make the choices shown there. Turning off personalized ads may not stop contextual ads, measurement, or fraud-prevention processing. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not itself delete account data stored by a service.

Retention and security

We keep account-linked information while needed to provide the service and handle legitimate operational or legal needs. Local app information can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling. We use service providers and reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Provider retention is also governed by their own policies.

Access and deletion requests

We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership. Do not email a child’s date of birth, passwords, or other sensitive information. A deletion request is handled by support; this page does not claim an automated deletion feature or promise a fixed completion period. Some records may be retained where law requires or permits it.

Changes and contact
